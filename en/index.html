About Us

A church rooted in Chuncheon since 2009

We are a branch church directly affiliated with Yoido Full Gospel Church in Seoul. For 17 years we have served our neighbors in Dongnae-myeon, Chuncheon, as a community of worship, healing, sharing and mission.

Worship

A community overflowing with the joy of salvation, prayer and praise.

Healing

A place where body, mind and spirit are restored.

Sharing & Mission

Loving our neighbors and sharing the gospel of Jesus Christ.

Serving Our Community

Onharu Children's Center (in planning)

Together with the international relief NGO Good People, we are preparing an after-school and evening care center for young elementary children, which will also create caregiving jobs for local young adults. The center will be open to all children regardless of religion.
